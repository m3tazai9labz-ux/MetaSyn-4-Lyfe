# Quick Start Guide

## How to Use This Fitness App Prompt System

This fitness app prompt system is designed to work with ChatGPT or other AI assistants to provide personalized fitness guidance.

### Getting Started

1. **Copy the System Prompt**
   - Navigate to `60-prompts/fitness-prompt.md`
   - Copy the entire prompt
   - Paste it into ChatGPT as a custom instruction or at the start of your conversation

2. **Set Your Goals**
   - Review the goal templates in `20-goals/goal-templates.md`
   - Decide on your primary fitness goal (weight loss, muscle gain, endurance, etc.)
   - Share this with the AI assistant

3. **Provide Your Context**
   - Current fitness level
   - Available equipment
   - Time commitment
   - Any limitations or health conditions
   - Dietary preferences

4. **Get Your Plan**
   - The AI will create a personalized workout plan
   - Receive nutrition guidance tailored to your goals
   - Get a progress tracking strategy

### Example Conversation Flow

**You**: "I'm a beginner looking to lose 20 pounds over the next 3 months. I have access to a gym and can work out 4 days per week. I have no injuries but haven't exercised regularly in over a year."

**AI** (using the fitness prompt): *[Will assess your situation and create a customized plan]*

### Ongoing Use

- **Weekly Check-ins**: Share your progress and get adjustments
- **Ask Questions**: Get clarification on exercises, nutrition, or techniques
- **Request Modifications**: If something isn't working, ask for alternatives
- **Track Progress**: Use the tracking guide in `50-progress/` to monitor your journey

### Reference Materials

- **Workout Templates**: See `30-workouts/workout-templates.md` for exercise ideas
- **Nutrition Guide**: See `40-nutrition/nutrition-guide.md` for meal planning
- **Progress Tracking**: See `50-progress/tracking-guide.md` for monitoring methods
- **Goal Templates**: See `20-goals/goal-templates.md` for goal-setting frameworks

### Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific** with your goals and constraints
2. **Be honest** about your current fitness level
3. **Provide feedback** on what's working and what isn't
4. **Ask questions** when you're unsure
5. **Stay consistent** with your plan
6. **Track progress** regularly

### Safety First

- Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting a new fitness program
- Listen to your body and report any pain or discomfort
- Focus on proper form over heavy weights or high intensity
- Don't skip warm-ups and cool-downs

## Sample Prompts to Get Started

### For Weight Loss
"I want to lose weight. I'm [height] and currently weigh [weight]. My goal is to lose [X] pounds. I can work out [X] days per week and have access to [equipment]. I have [time] per session."

### For Muscle Gain
"I want to build muscle. I'm [height] and currently weigh [weight]. My goal is to gain [X] pounds of muscle. I can train [X] days per week with access to [equipment]. I've been training for [duration]."

### For General Fitness
"I want to get in better shape and establish a consistent exercise routine. I have [equipment] available and can dedicate [time] per week to fitness. What would you recommend?"

### For Athletic Performance
"I play [sport] and want to improve my performance. Specifically, I want to work on [specific aspect]. I can train [X] days per week. What training approach would you suggest?"
