# Fitness Goal Templates

## Common Fitness Goals

### 1. Weight Loss
**Target**: Lose [X] pounds in [Y] weeks
- **Strategy**: Caloric deficit + cardio + strength training
- **Nutrition**: Focus on whole foods, portion control
- **Exercise**: 3-5 cardio sessions + 2-3 strength sessions per week
- **Tracking**: Weekly weigh-ins, body measurements, progress photos

### 2. Muscle Gain
**Target**: Gain [X] pounds of muscle in [Y] months
- **Strategy**: Progressive overload + caloric surplus + adequate protein
- **Nutrition**: 1g protein per lb bodyweight, slight caloric surplus
- **Exercise**: 4-5 strength training sessions per week
- **Tracking**: Body composition, strength metrics, measurements

### 3. Improved Endurance
**Target**: Run [X] miles or complete [Y] activity
- **Strategy**: Progressive distance/time increases
- **Nutrition**: Adequate carbohydrates for energy
- **Exercise**: 3-4 endurance sessions per week with gradual progression
- **Tracking**: Distance, time, heart rate, perceived exertion

### 4. General Health & Fitness
**Target**: Establish consistent exercise routine and healthy habits
- **Strategy**: Balanced approach to fitness and nutrition
- **Nutrition**: Balanced diet with variety
- **Exercise**: Mix of cardio, strength, and flexibility work
- **Tracking**: Consistency, energy levels, overall well-being

### 5. Athletic Performance
**Target**: Improve specific sport or activity performance
- **Strategy**: Sport-specific training + foundational fitness
- **Nutrition**: Tailored to sport demands
- **Exercise**: Sport practice + complementary conditioning
- **Tracking**: Performance metrics, skill development

## SMART Goal Framework

When setting fitness goals, ensure they are:
- **Specific**: Clearly defined objective
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable progress indicators
- **Achievable**: Realistic given current fitness level
- **Relevant**: Aligned with overall health priorities
- **Time-bound**: Specific deadline or timeframe

## Progress Milestones

### Short-term (1-4 weeks)
- Establish routine consistency
- Learn proper exercise form
- Develop healthy habits

### Mid-term (1-3 months)
- Visible progress toward goals
- Strength/endurance improvements
- Habit solidification

### Long-term (3-12 months)
- Significant transformation
- Goal achievement
- Lifestyle integration
