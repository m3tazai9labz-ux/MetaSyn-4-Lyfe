# Workout Plan Templates

## Beginner Full-Body Workout (3x per week)

### Workout A
1. **Warm-up** (5-10 min)
   - Light cardio + dynamic stretching

2. **Main Workout**
   - Squats: 3 sets x 10 reps
   - Push-ups (modified if needed): 3 sets x 8-12 reps
   - Dumbbell Rows: 3 sets x 10 reps per arm
   - Plank: 3 sets x 20-30 seconds
   - Walking Lunges: 2 sets x 10 reps per leg

3. **Cool-down** (5-10 min)
   - Static stretching

### Workout B
1. **Warm-up** (5-10 min)
   - Light cardio + dynamic stretching

2. **Main Workout**
   - Deadlifts (or Romanian Deadlifts): 3 sets x 10 reps
   - Overhead Press: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
   - Lat Pulldowns or Pull-up Negatives: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
   - Step-ups: 3 sets x 10 reps per leg
   - Bicycle Crunches: 3 sets x 15 reps per side

3. **Cool-down** (5-10 min)
   - Static stretching

**Schedule**: Alternate A and B, with at least one rest day between workouts

## Intermediate Upper/Lower Split (4x per week)

### Upper Body Day
1. Bench Press: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
2. Bent-Over Rows: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
3. Overhead Press: 3 sets x 10 reps
4. Pull-ups or Lat Pulldowns: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
5. Dumbbell Flyes: 3 sets x 12 reps
6. Bicep Curls: 3 sets x 12 reps
7. Tricep Dips: 3 sets x 10-12 reps

### Lower Body Day
1. Squats: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
2. Romanian Deadlifts: 4 sets x 10 reps
3. Leg Press: 3 sets x 12 reps
4. Walking Lunges: 3 sets x 10 reps per leg
5. Leg Curls: 3 sets x 12 reps
6. Calf Raises: 4 sets x 15 reps
7. Hanging Leg Raises: 3 sets x 12 reps

**Schedule**: Upper/Lower/Rest/Upper/Lower/Rest/Rest

---

## Advanced Push/Pull/Legs (5-6x per week)

### Push Day (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)
1. Flat Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
2. Incline Dumbbell Press: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
3. Overhead Press: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
4. Lateral Raises: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
5. Tricep Pushdowns: 3 sets x 12 reps
6. Overhead Tricep Extension: 3 sets x 12 reps

### Pull Day (Back, Biceps)
1. Deadlifts: 4 sets x 5-6 reps
2. Pull-ups: 4 sets x 8-12 reps
3. Barbell Rows: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
4. Face Pulls: 3 sets x 15 reps
5. Barbell Curls: 3 sets x 10 reps
6. Hammer Curls: 3 sets x 12 reps

### Legs Day
1. Squats: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
2. Front Squats: 3 sets x 10 reps
3. Leg Press: 3 sets x 12 reps
4. Romanian Deadlifts: 4 sets x 10 reps
5. Leg Curls: 3 sets x 12 reps
6. Calf Raises: 4 sets x 15-20 reps
7. Ab Wheel Rollouts: 3 sets x 10-12 reps

**Schedule**: Push/Pull/Legs/Rest/Push/Pull/Legs or Push/Pull/Legs/Rest/Repeat

## Cardio Recommendations

### For Weight Loss
- 3-5 sessions per week
- 30-45 minutes moderate intensity
- Options: Running, cycling, swimming, rowing, elliptical

### For Endurance
- 4-6 sessions per week
- Mix of long steady-state and interval training
- Progressive distance/time increases

### For Heart Health
- 150 minutes moderate or 75 minutes vigorous per week
- Can be broken into smaller sessions
- Any aerobic activity

---

## Notes on Progression

- **Increase weight** when you can complete all sets with good form
- **Add reps** if increasing weight isn't available
- **Reduce rest time** to increase intensity
- **Add sets** for more volume
- **Track workouts** to monitor progress
- **Deload every 4-6 weeks** to prevent overtraining:
  - Reduce weight by 40-60% while maintaining same exercises
  - Keep same rep ranges but reduce total sets by 30-50%
  - Or maintain weight but reduce volume (fewer sets/reps)
  - Focus on recovery, sleep, and form refinement
  - Return to regular training after 1 week
