# User Conversation Templates

## Initial Assessment Questions

### Basic Information
```
Hello! I'm your AI fitness coach. To create the best plan for you, I need to understand your current situation. Let me ask you a few questions:

1. What is your primary fitness goal?
   - [ ] Weight loss
   - [ ] Muscle gain
   - [ ] Improved endurance/cardio
   - [ ] General health and fitness
   - [ ] Athletic performance
   - [ ] Other: __________

2. What is your current fitness level?
   - [ ] Beginner (little to no exercise experience)
   - [ ] Intermediate (exercise regularly for several months)
   - [ ] Advanced (consistent training for years)

3. Do you have any injuries, health conditions, or physical limitations?
   
4. What equipment do you have access to?
   - [ ] Full gym
   - [ ] Home gym (dumbbells, bench, etc.)
   - [ ] Bodyweight only
   - [ ] Other: __________

5. How much time can you dedicate to workouts?
   - Days per week: ____
   - Minutes per session: ____

6. What are your dietary preferences or restrictions?
```

### Follow-up Questions for Weight Loss
```
Great! Since your goal is weight loss, I have a few more questions:

1. What is your current weight and target weight?
2. What is your timeline for achieving this goal?
3. Have you tried any diets or weight loss programs before? What worked or didn't work?
4. Do you prefer cardio, strength training, or a mix of both?
5. Are there any foods you absolutely cannot give up?
```

### Follow-up Questions for Muscle Gain
```
Excellent! For muscle gain, I need to know:

1. What is your current weight and target weight?
2. How long have you been training?
3. What does your current workout routine look like?
4. What is your current daily protein intake?
5. Are you willing to adjust your diet significantly?
6. Which muscle groups are you most interested in developing?
```

### Follow-up Questions for Endurance
```
Perfect! For endurance training:

1. What is your target event or distance? (e.g., 5K, marathon, cycling century)
2. When is your target date?
3. What is your current fitness baseline? (e.g., can run 1 mile, cycle 10 miles)
4. How many days per week can you train?
5. Do you prefer running, cycling, swimming, or other activities?
```

## Common User Questions and Answers

### "How long until I see results?"
```
Great question! The timeline for results varies based on your goal:

**Weight Loss:**
- Scale changes: 1-2 weeks
- Visual changes: 4-6 weeks
- Significant transformation: 8-12 weeks

**Muscle Gain:**
- Strength increases: 2-4 weeks
- Visual changes: 6-8 weeks
- Significant growth: 12+ weeks

**Endurance:**
- Performance improvements: 2-3 weeks
- Cardiovascular adaptations: 4-6 weeks
- Major capacity increases: 8-12 weeks

Remember, consistency is key! Small daily actions compound into major transformations over time.
```

### "I'm too sore to work out. What should I do?"
```
Muscle soreness (DOMS) is normal, especially when starting out. Here's how to handle it:

**If soreness is moderate (5-6/10):**
- Proceed with your workout but at reduced intensity
- Focus on different muscle groups
- Do active recovery (light cardio, stretching)

**If soreness is severe (7+/10):**
- Take an extra rest day
- Do gentle movement (walking, swimming)
- Focus on recovery (sleep, hydration, nutrition)

**To reduce future soreness:**
- Proper warm-up and cool-down
- Gradual progression (don't do too much too soon)
- Stay hydrated
- Get adequate sleep
- Consider foam rolling or stretching

Pain is different from soreness. If you feel sharp pain or joint issues, stop and consult a healthcare provider.
```

### "I'm not losing weight despite following the plan. Help!"
```
I understand your frustration. Let's troubleshoot together:

**Questions to consider:**
1. Are you tracking your food intake accurately (including drinks, snacks, cooking oils)?
2. How's your sleep quality (less than 7 hours can affect weight loss)?
3. Are you under high stress?
4. How long have you been at the same weight?
5. Have you noticed any non-scale victories (measurements, energy, strength)?

**Common issues:**
- Underestimating calorie intake (use a food scale)
- Overestimating calorie burn from exercise
- Not enough protein (reduces muscle loss)
- Water retention (can mask fat loss)
- Need more patience (2 weeks isn't always enough)

**Next steps:**
Let's adjust your approach:
1. [Specific recommendation]
2. [Specific recommendation]
3. [Specific recommendation]

Weight loss isn't always linear. Trust the process and focus on consistency!
```
