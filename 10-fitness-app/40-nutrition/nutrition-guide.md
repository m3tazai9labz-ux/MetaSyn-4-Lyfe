# Nutrition Guide

## Macronutrient Basics

### Protein
- **Purpose**: Muscle building and repair, satiety
- **Sources**: Chicken, fish, beef, eggs, dairy, legumes, tofu
- **Recommendation**: 0.7-1.0g per lb of bodyweight
- **For muscle gain**: 1.0-1.2g per lb of bodyweight

### Carbohydrates
- **Purpose**: Energy, workout fuel, recovery
- **Sources**: Rice, oats, potatoes, fruits, vegetables, whole grains
- **Recommendation**: Varies based on activity level and goals
- **For fat loss**: Moderate (30-40% of calories)
- **For muscle gain/performance**: Higher (40-50% of calories)

### Fats
- **Purpose**: Hormone production, nutrient absorption, satiety
- **Sources**: Avocados, nuts, olive oil, fatty fish, eggs
- **Recommendation**: 0.3-0.5g per lb of bodyweight
- **Minimum**: 20-25% of total calories

---

## Calorie Guidelines

### For Weight Loss
- Calculate maintenance calories (TDEE)
- Create deficit of 300-500 calories per day
- Aim for 0.5-1.5 lbs loss per week
- Prioritize protein to preserve muscle

### For Muscle Gain
- Calculate maintenance calories (TDEE)
- Create surplus of 200-500 calories per day
- Aim for 0.5-1 lb gain per week
- Higher gains likely include more fat

### For Maintenance
- Eat at TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)
- Adjust based on activity level
- Focus on nutrient-dense foods

## Sample Meal Plans

### Weight Loss Meal Plan (~1,800 calories)

**Breakfast**
- 3 scrambled eggs with spinach
- 1 slice whole grain toast
- 1/2 avocado
- Black coffee or tea

**Snack**
- Greek yogurt (non-fat)
- Handful of berries

**Lunch**
- Grilled chicken breast (6 oz)
- Large mixed salad with olive oil dressing
- Quinoa (1/2 cup cooked)

**Snack**
- Apple with almond butter (1 tbsp)

**Dinner**
- Baked salmon (5 oz)
- Roasted vegetables
- Sweet potato (medium)

**Macros**: ~140g protein, ~160g carbs, ~60g fat

---

### Muscle Gain Meal Plan (~2,800 calories)

**Breakfast**
- 4 whole eggs + 2 egg whites
- Oatmeal (1 cup dry) with banana
- Whole milk (8 oz)

**Snack**
- Protein shake with banana and peanut butter

**Lunch**
- Chicken breast (8 oz)
- Brown rice (1.5 cups cooked)
- Broccoli
- Olive oil drizzle

**Snack**
- Greek yogurt with granola and honey

**Dinner**
- Lean beef (8 oz)
- Pasta (1.5 cups cooked)
- Side salad

**Evening Snack**
- Casein protein shake or cottage cheese with berries

**Macros**: ~210g protein, ~300g carbs, ~90g fat

## Meal Timing Strategies

### Pre-Workout (1-2 hours before)
- Moderate carbs + moderate protein
- Low fat (digests slower)
- Example: Oatmeal with protein powder and banana

### Post-Workout (within 1-2 hours)
- Protein for muscle repair
- Carbs to replenish glycogen
- Example: Chicken with rice and vegetables

### Throughout the Day
- Eat every 3-4 hours for stable energy
- Include protein at each meal
- Don't skip meals

---

## Hydration Guidelines

- **Base recommendation**: 64 oz (8 cups) per day minimum
- **Active individuals**: Add 12-16 oz per hour of exercise
- **Hot weather**: Increase further
- **Indicators**: Clear/light yellow urine, adequate energy

---

## Supplements (Optional)

### Worth Considering:
- **Protein Powder**: Convenient protein source
- **Creatine**: 5g daily for strength/muscle gains
- **Vitamin D**: If deficient or limited sun exposure
- **Omega-3**: If low fish intake
- **Multivitamin**: Insurance for nutrient gaps

### Not Essential But Popular:
- Pre-workout (caffeine main benefit)
- BCAAs (unnecessary if protein intake is adequate)
- Fat burners (not magic pills)

---

## General Nutrition Tips

1. **Prioritize whole foods**: 80-90% of diet
2. **Meal prep**: Prepare meals in advance for consistency
3. **Track initially**: Use app to understand portions and macros
4. **Stay flexible**: Allow for occasional treats (10-20% of diet)
5. **Listen to your body**: Adjust based on hunger, energy, performance
6. **Be consistent**: Long-term adherence matters most
7. **Adjust as needed**: Monitor progress and modify accordingly

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Cutting calories too aggressively
- Neglecting protein intake
- Eliminating entire food groups unnecessarily
- Not eating enough vegetables
- Poor meal timing around workouts
- Not drinking enough water
- Expecting overnight results
- Following overly restrictive diets
